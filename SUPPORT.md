Email us for help with ChemCreate, bug reports, or privacy questions. Please do not post confidential, proprietary, unpublished, personal, or patent-sensitive chemical structures in public GitHub issues. If a sample is needed, use a small example that you have the right to share, or contact us privately first.

When reporting a problem, include:

1. ChemCreate version and build number;
2. macOS, iOS, or iPadOS version and device model;
3. the steps that reproduce the problem;
4. what you expected and what happened;
5. whether the document is `.chemcreate`, MOL, SDF, or SMILES, if relevant.

ChemCreate supports Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later, iPhones running iOS 17 or later, and iPads running iPadOS 17 or later. `.chemcreate` is the complete editable document format. MOL, SDF, and SMILES are chemistry interchange formats and may not represent all drawing annotations. The app should warn before an export that would lose such content.

For information about document storage and local NMR processing, read the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). Third-party license texts are in [Third-Party Notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt).
