# ChemCreate Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026  
**Developer and contact:** ZEYI CAI, an independent developer — [quickiee@foxmail.com](mailto:quickiee@foxmail.com)

This policy describes how the ChemCreate app for macOS, iOS, and iPadOS handles information. A [Simplified Chinese version](PRIVACY.zh-CN.md) is also available.

## Data handled by the app

ChemCreate does not require an account and does not collect personal data from the app. The current app has no developer-operated cloud service, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. It does not send molecular structures, documents, or NMR results to the developer.

Chemical structures, `.chemcreate` documents, MOL/SDF files, SMILES text, annotations, clipboard contents, and NMR predictions are processed on the user's device. The user chooses where to save, export, or share files. If a user chooses iCloud Drive or another File Provider, that provider may sync or process files under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Local storage and deletion

ChemCreate stores preferences such as interface language and text size in local Apple platform settings. The app also writes NMR warning codes and limited failure descriptions to its local cache. Those warning files are not transmitted to the developer; the app removes files after 15 days and limits the warning-log directory to 5 MiB.

Users manage and delete their documents through Finder, Files, iCloud Drive, or the File Provider they selected. Preferences remain on the device until changed or removed under the platform's data-management behavior. Removing a document from ChemCreate does not necessarily delete a copy held by another app or storage provider.

## Contacting support

If you email [quickiee@foxmail.com](mailto:quickiee@foxmail.com), the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include. That information is used to respond to and manage your request, is kept only while needed for support or a legal obligation, and is not used for advertising. You may request deletion by emailing the same address. Please avoid sending confidential, proprietary, unpublished, or patent-sensitive chemical structures unless necessary and you have the right to share them.

GitHub issues are public. If you choose to post an issue, its content is visible to others and is handled by GitHub under its own policies. Use email for private matters.

## This public repository

These documents are hosted by GitHub. Opening a GitHub page may provide GitHub with technical request information such as an IP address; GitHub's [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) governs that processing. The ChemCreate app does not embed GitHub tracking or use this repository as an app data service.

## Changes and questions

If app data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated. For privacy questions or requests, contact [quickiee@foxmail.com](mailto:quickiee@foxmail.com).
